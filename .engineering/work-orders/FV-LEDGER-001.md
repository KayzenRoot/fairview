# FV-LEDGER-001 | Pure synthetic order-event ledger

**Admitted implementation scope, not a live or persistent financial ledger.** Protected-main base: `37ab859aa8e2d8fff02ab910ac7e948ce331e0ca`; native Git/Node22/pinned GEF, 20 modules with bootstrap, policy, clock and market-data active. The ledger has only the active `policy` as a direct prerequisite. This round implements deterministic, in-memory immutable order-event fixtures without real orders, database installation, server, broker API, credentials or authoritative reconciliation.

**Exact allowlist:** this WO, `.engineering/context-locks/FV-LEDGER-001.md`, `src/ledger/simulation.mjs`, `tests/ledger/simulation.test.mjs`, `harness/modules.json` (ledger state and owned test glob only), `tests/bootstrap/impact.test.mjs`, `tests/bootstrap/evidence-contract.test.mjs`, `docs/architecture/modules/ledger.md`, `docs/architecture/MODULE-READINESS-AND-IMPLEMENTATION-R10.md` and a small truth/status appendix in `docs/architecture/LEDGER-RISK-EXECUTION-R3.md`. No other module source, production DB, workflow, GEF pin, canonical checkpoint or security gate changes.

**Contract:** synthetic `OrderIntentV0` must have exact scoped tenant, account, venue, instrument, order identity, side, positive canonical integer size and an independently evaluated **invented** policy request from the active policy evaluator. A `DEMO_ELIGIBLE` or `PAPER_ELIGIBLE` fictional policy result may initialize an immutable in-memory event log, but NEVER authorizes any order. Full fixed-schema events have unique IDs, timestamps supplied as literal fixtures, type, exact fill quantities, complete simulated reconciliation markers and source labels. Maintain local simulated idempotency and deterministic replay; duplicate IDs with identical facts are ignored, conflicting duplicate IDs DENY/LOCK. Require explicit `MAY_HAVE_SENT` before synthetic ACK/partial fill, unknown after lost ACK with no blind resend, cancellation-requested is not canceled, and late fills during cancel count. An external outcome never becomes known merely because a local ledger transition succeeded. `RECONCILE` requires an explicit complete scoped synthetic receipt; incomplete history locks instead of clearing unknowns. Never claim real persistence, cross-process idempotency, venue-authenticated receipt or exactly-once transmission.

**Actual nonempty owned tests:** create/deny by scope/policy; duplicate intent and event conflicts; crash after synthetic pre-send marker; lost ACK/partial fills; cancel/fill race; cumulative bounds and replay order; unknown/reconciliation incomplete; consistent whole-log deterministic replay; frozen source snapshot and denial of all `REAL_VENDOR` events, malformed IDs and quantities. Run exact-head 4/4 hosted CI including Windows PS5.1; preserve proposed ADR-004 status and all other PLANNED owners. Owner self-audit NOT_INDEPENDENT. Financial durability in PostgreSQL, risk kernel, independent legal rights and actual broker receipts remain separately gated future WOs.
