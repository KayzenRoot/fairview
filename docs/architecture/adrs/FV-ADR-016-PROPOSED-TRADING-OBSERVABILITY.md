# FV-ADR-016 | Privacy-bounded trading telemetry, incident provenance and alert routing

**Status: PROPOSED_NOT_ADOPTED.** FV-DISC-001 Round 8 documentation only. No telemetry collector, dashboard/alert contact, production secrets or financial logs have been installed or accessed.

## Decision proposal
Use a **separate, non-authoritative** future observability plane. `TradingTraceEnvelopeV0` traces coarse stage transitions from tick/capture to risk, durable intent, possible dispatch, venue ACK/fill and reconciliation. `LatencyMeasurementV0` measures only same-monotonic-domain intervals and exposes a histogram population and sufficient `n`, missing/dropped counts, uncertainty and separate synthetic/demo/observed classes; do NOT subtract cross-host monotonic timestamps or infer remote one-way latency. `TelemetryQualityV0` tracks bounded exporter capacity, collector failure, high-cardinality overflow, clock health, retention and redaction quality. `IncidentEnvelopeV0` and `OperatorAlertRouteV0` record private references to authoritative Ledger/Portfolio/Risk incidents with independently configured routing, escalation and immutable kill epoch, not broker-trade details or customer account identifiers in alert text.

Proposed candidates: OpenTelemetry histogram/exemplar and trace correlation, Prometheus classic/native histograms and `histogram_quantile` where resolution/sample limits permit, Grafana or Alertmanager coarse severity-based contact points and notification routing. Use only bounded low-cardinality metric attributes (stage, module, mode, quality class); place sensitive/high-cardinality identifiers in restricted trace stores as opaque references, never metric labels or public CI. Validate actual Rust OpenTelemetry SDK version/maturity before adopting. Official sources: https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/signals/metrics/ ; https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/otel/metrics/data-model/ ; https://prometheus.io/docs/practices/histograms/ ; https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/latest/alerting/fundamentals/notifications/ .

## Independent trading-safety boundary
No synchronous network exporter or overloaded collector may block a time-critical Ledger, Risk or Execution transaction. Missing optional diagnostics means observable `DEGRADED`; missing mandatory independent audit evidence/oversight can trigger a **separate** fail-closed operational PAUSE by policy without trusting Alertmanager as risk engine. Persist financial safety state and incident provenance independently before best-effort notification. Grafana/Alertmanager ACK, silences, collector restart, UI refresh or AI advice **cannot** mark external fills reconciled, override a kill or release held collateral.

## Rejected shortcuts and acceptance
Reject a metric time-series per order/account/wallet, raw tick/customer financial content in hosted public CI, a synthetic p99 from sparse/mixed populations, unmeasured submillisecond cross-host latency, and "green dashboards" proving a venue is reconciled. Future admitted observability WO needs dummy/redacted privacy tests, exporter queue saturation, alert route failure, short-lived exemplar correlation, trace no-secret leak, clock-domain mismatch, insufficient-tail benchmark and mandatory local audit failure drills; independent security review and FV-BOOT-001 FULL first.
